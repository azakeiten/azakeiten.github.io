About us

From an exhibition to a club.

We started as a team building an interactive exhibition, the Azabu Economic Exhibition, for our school festival. Visitors earn and trade chips through quizzes and card games, experiencing how an economy works by playing it.

Building it taught us that economics is far more interesting when you move it yourself. So we took the name AZAKEI (short for Azabu Keizai, "Azabu Economics") and expanded beyond the festival.

Partner with us

Help us make economics fun.

We welcome companies and organizations that share our goal of making economics accessible and exciting for students and our community.

  • Sponsorship of the exhibition and contest participation
  • Prizes or materials for the exhibition
  • Guest talks and dialogues at the festival
  • Mentoring for business plans and company analysis
  • Joint events, collaborations, and media coverage
