About us

From an exhibition to a club.

We started as a team building an interactive exhibition, the Azabu Economic Exhibition, for our school festival. Visitors earn and trade chips through quizzes and card games, experiencing how an economy works by playing it.

Building it taught us that economics is far more interesting when you move it yourself. So we took the name AZAKEI (short for Azabu Keizai, "Azabu Economics") and expanded beyond the festival.

Contests

Testing ourselves beyond school.

01

Business contests

Find a social problem, design a business that solves it, and turn it into a business plan and pitch.

02

Nikkei Stock League

An investment and research competition run by Nikkei Inc. We choose a theme, analyze companies, build a virtual portfolio, and write a report.

03

Entrepreneur auditions

Pitch our ideas directly to entrepreneurs and judges, and learn whether they could really work.

04

Research papers

Ask a question about the economy, dig into data and sources, and write it up as a paper.
